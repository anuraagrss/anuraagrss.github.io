behavior: click fires the mocked equivalent of four parallel Firestore queries (flights/airports/photos/people joined by country_id, same as before). Instead of fading in, each field resolves through a Solari split-flap animation — text rows (destination, flight rows, airport codes, people names) flap character-by-character into place, cascading left→right and row→row. Once the last row settles, photos (which can't be represented as flap-text) and portraits fade in below the board. Rows with no data show a dim placeholder instead of flapping empty.
